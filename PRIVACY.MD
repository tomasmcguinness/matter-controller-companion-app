# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 23rd September 2026

## 1. No Data Collection
We do not collect, store, log, or share any personal information or user data. 

## 2. No Third-Party Libraries
Our application does not use any third-party software development kits (SDKs), tracking libraries, analytics services, or advertising networks.

## 3. Permissions
The app does not request or require access to sensitive device features or data (such as your location, contacts, camera, or device ID).

## 4. Notifications (Optional)
Notifications are off unless you turn them on in the app's Settings. When you do:

- The app asks for permission to show notifications and gets a device token from Apple's Push Notification service (APNs). The token only identifies the app on your phone for delivering notifications.
- The app sends that token, along with the push relay username and password you entered for each controller, to your own Matter controllers on your local network. They are stored on those controllers, not by us.
- When a controller sends you a notification, its text and your device token pass through our push relay to Apple. The relay doesn't store either; it keeps operational logs (time, the sending controller's IP address, the result, and the first few characters of the token) to keep the service running.

Turning notifications off tells your controllers to delete the token.
